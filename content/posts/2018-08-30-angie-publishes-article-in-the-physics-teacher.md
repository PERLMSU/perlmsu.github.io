Title: Angie publishes article in The Physics Teacher
Date: 2018-08-30
Slug: angie-publishes-article-in-the-physics-teacher

Angie Little and colleague published a paper in The Physics Teacher titled [*On the Importance of Engaging Students in Crafting Definitions*](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.5051144).

Abstract: In this paper we describe an activity for engaging students in crafting definitions. We explore the strengths of this particular activity as well as the broader implications of engaging students in crafting definitions more generally.
