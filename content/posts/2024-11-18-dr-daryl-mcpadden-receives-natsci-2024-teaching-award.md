Title: Dr. Daryl McPadden Receives NatSci 2024 Teaching Award
Date: 2024-11-18
Slug: dr-daryl-mcpadden-receives-natsci-2024-teaching-award

Dr. Daryl McPadden was awarded the 2024 College of Natural Science Undergraduate Teaching Award. Dar. McPadden is an assistant professor in the Department of Physics and Astronomy and Physics Education Research Lab. 

This award recognizes Dr. McPadden for her vast instructional efforts in Physics and Astronomy program. This work includes designing, developing, implementing, and coordinating the innovative PCubed and EMPCubed courses and the teaching of her own sections of these courses. Dr. McPadden's scholarship in physics education research underpins many of these efforts. 

Her work in curriculum design and implementation is complimented by her efforts to build inclusive classroom experiences and supportve instructional materials and processes, so that all students can be successful in physics coursework.
