Title: Dr. Daryl McPadden has been awarded the College of Natural Science’s Meritorious Faculty Award
Date: 2026-04-16
Slug: dr-daryl-mcpadden-has-been-awarded-the-college-of-natural-sciences-meritorious-f

![Photo - MSU College of Natural Science Dean Eric Hegg with Meritorious Faculty Award winner Assistant Professor Daryl McPadden at the 2026 Alumni Awards.](/assets/img/news/2026-04-alumni-awards-mcpadden-web.jpg)

The Physics Education Research Lab at Michigan State University is proud to announce that **Prof. Daryl McPadden** has been awarded the **College of Natural Science’s Meritorious Faculty Award** in recognition of her outstanding contributions to physics education during the [2026 Alumni Awards Program](https://natsci.msu.edu/news/2026/2026-04-alumni-awards-honor-many-in-college-community.aspx).

The Meritorious Faculty Award was presented to [Daryl McPadden](https://directory.natsci.msu.edu/directory/Profiles/Person/102065), an assistant professor in the [Department of Physics and Astronomy](https://pa.msu.edu/) and a nationally recognized physics education researcher. McPadden’s work focuses on creating inclusive, active learning environments and reducing barriers in STEM education, particularly for students with disabilities.

Congratulations, Daryl, on this well-deserved honor!
