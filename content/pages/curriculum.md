## Projects and Practices in Physics (P-Cubed)

P-Cubed is a problem-based introductory mechanics course in which students work in groups on complex problems, including computational ones. Course materials are available on the [P-Cubed wiki](https://www.msuperl.org/wikis/pcubed/doku.php). See [Research Projects](/research/projects/) for research on the course.
