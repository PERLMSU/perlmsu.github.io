## Join our mailing list

Interested in PERL events and seminars? Consider joining our mailing list. Send an email to [listserv@list.msu.edu](mailto:listserv@list.msu.edu?body=JOIN%20perl) with the message: `JOIN perl`

Once approved, you will begin receiving emails about PERL [events and seminars](/events/).

## Undergraduate research

Undergraduate students interested in working with PERL on research projects are encouraged to contact any [member of the PERL faculty](/people/#faculty). Our group has a number of research projects for undergraduates for which students may volunteer or earn course credit (through PHY 405). A limited number of paid positions are typically available after one semester of either volunteering or taking course credit.

## Doctoral research

Graduate students interested in exploring research with PERL are encouraged to contact [all members of the PERL faculty](/people/#faculty) as we collectively discuss taking on new doctoral students. This includes current and future MSU students.

## PHY 905: Physics Education Research

Interested undergraduate and graduate students are encouraged to take PHY 905: Physics Education Research, a special topics course that surveys the state of the research field.
